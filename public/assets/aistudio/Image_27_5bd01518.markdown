## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an intentional, mindful editorial atmosphere. It balances the meditative calm of wellness journals with the typographic rigor of high-end publishing. 

The aesthetic is organic, grounded, and quiet. Interfaces prioritize spaciousness, clear temporal rhythms, and tactile warmth over high-frequency feedback loops. A persistent, understated timeline motif runs throughout, grounding interactions in moments and progression rather than urgency.

- **Movement:** Organic Modernism meets Editorial Serenity.
- **Tone:** Grounded, contemplative, refined, patient.
- **Motion Philosophy:** Ambient pacing with lingering ease-outs, subtle opacity shifts, and deliberate linear progression.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from tactile paper stocks, dried botanicals, and patinated metals. Contrast is achieved thoughtfully without stark polarities.

- **Linen (`#F4F1EA`):** Canvas background. Emits zero digital glare and mimics heavyweight unbleached paper.
- **Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated cards, active panels, and floating sheets to generate distinct tactile depth against Linen.
- **Deep Pine (`#2F3E34`):** Primary ink for titles, body copy, key iconography, and reversed dark sections. Provides authoritative contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Stone (`#8A8F85`):** Muted metadata, captions, inactive timeline nodes, and subtle dividers.
- **Sage (`#9CAF88`):** Restful wash tone used for soft badges, secondary pills, progress bars, and passive timeline tracks.
- **Key Brass (`#C9A24B`):** Warm luminescent accent used purposefully for dates, focal callouts, active milestones, and primary action surfaces. Primary brass buttons always take Deep Pine typography for grounded legibility.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on Plus Jakarta Sans, combining geometric cleanliness with gentle, humanist stroke variations. 

- **Headings:** Set in medium to semi-bold weights with slight negative letter tracking to retain editorial structure without appearing aggressive.
- **Body:** Generous line heights (`lineHeight: 26px` on 16px body) allow long-form reflection and reading without cognitive fatigue.
- **Dates & Highlights:** Often set using `label-sm` or `caption` with expanded tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) in uppercase Key Brass, providing a disciplined journal-like ledger layout.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial grid with breathing room at every breakpoint.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive layout on desktop with a maximum reading container width of 1140px; collapses to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Rooted in consistent multiples of 8px (via `space-*`). Generous page margins ensure cards never touch screen edges on handheld viewports.
- **Timeline Alignment:** On desktop and tablet, an offset left rail (48px wide) serves as the anchor track for the subtle timeline motif, aligning milestone nodes precisely with adjacent card headers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and warm, avoiding harsh directional shadows or cold synthetic glows.

- **Tonal Layers:** The primary contrast occurs between canvas (`#F4F1EA`) and surface (`#FFFFFF`). Secondary panels or sub-sections employ subtle Sage washes (`rgba(156, 175, 136, 0.12)`) or deep inverted sections in `#2F3E34`.
- **Warm Shadow:** Cards and floating modules utilize a diffused, low-opacity warm umber drop shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(47, 62, 52, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(47, 62, 52, 0.03)`.
- **Borders:** Crisp White cards feature an ultra-delicate outline to preserve edge definition against Linen: `1px solid rgba(156, 175, 136, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances soft contours with structured integrity.

- **Cards & Surfaces:** Standardized to a generous 20px (`1.25rem`) border radius, imparting an organic, river-stone softness.
- **Controls & Small Badges:** Form inputs, buttons, and milestone badges maintain matching rounded curvature or fully pill-shaped contours for ergonomic touch interactions.
- **Timeline Rail:** Connective timeline lines utilize rounded terminal caps (`stroke-linecap: round`) and 8px circular anchor nodes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Key Brass (`#C9A24B`) with Deep Pine (`#2F3E34`) text in `label-md`. Roundedness is 12px or pill-shaped. Soft hover transitions with a gentle brightness scale (`filter: brightness(0.96)`) and 150ms ease-out.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with a 1px border in Sage (`#9CAF88`) or Deep Pine text.
- **Subtle Surface:** Sage at 15% opacity with Deep Pine typography.

### Cards & Content Modules
- Surfaces are Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 20px border radius, warm drop shadow, and a 1px border in `rgba(156, 175, 136, 0.2)`. 
- Padding ranges from `space-lg` (24px) on mobile to `space-xl` (40px) on desktop to guarantee uncluttered interior space.

### Chips & Badges
- Background in Sage wash (`rgba(156, 175, 136, 0.18)`), Deep Pine text, 12px vertical alignment padding, and soft rounded ends.
- Highlighting chips (e.g., active dates or milestones) use Key Brass outline or soft Brass wash with Deep Pine text.

### Persistent Timeline Motif
- A continuous, 2px vertical guide wire in `rgba(138, 143, 133, 0.25)` or Sage connects content chronologically.
- Milestone nodes are 10px circular dots: hollow with a Sage border for pending states, solid Key Brass with an inner white center for the active state, and Deep Pine for completed records.

### Progress Indicators & Sliders
- Tracks are finished in soft Sage at 20% opacity.
- Fill bars transition smoothly in Key Brass or Deep Pine with gentle, non-linear easing.

### Input Fields
- Background in Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), border in Stone (`#8A8F85`) at 30% opacity, 12px corner radius, and Deep Pine text.
- Focus state softens the border to Key Brass (`#C9A24B`) accompanied by a subtle 2px brass halo (`rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Circular or soft-corner checks with Deep Pine fills and warm white icons.
- Inactive states remain bordered by Stone with zero fill to maintain simplicity.

### Motion & Transitions
- Micro-interactions use slow, meditative durations (300ms–500ms) with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. 
- Countdown and numerical transitions avoid rapid tumbling; they resolve through serene cross-fades and smooth, calm opacity slides.
